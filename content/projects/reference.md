The pages below are the reference catalogs behind [Song of Heroic Lands](/projects/sohl/) and [HârnMaster 3](/projects/hm3/). Every item, skill, trait, and ability that appears on a character sheet or creature stat block is defined in one of these catalogs.

# Characteristics

The intrinsic qualities of characters and creatures: what they can _do_, what they _are_, and what they _suffer_.

- [Skills](/sohl/skill/) — learned capabilities with trainable scores.
- [Traits](/sohl/trait/) — innate physical and mental qualities.
- [Mysteries](/sohl/mystery/) — religious and philosophical mysteries available to clerics, philosophers, and initiates.
- [Mystical Abilities](/sohl/mysticalability/) — arcane talents, invocations, and other supernatural capabilities.
- [Afflictions](/sohl/affliction/) — injuries, illnesses, curses, and other lasting adverse conditions.

# Possessions

Everything a character might carry, wield, wear, drink, or store.

- [Armor](/sohl/armorgear/) — head, torso, and limb protection across all materials.
- [Weapons](/sohl/weapongear/) — melee, thrown, and missile armaments.
- [Projectiles](/sohl/projectilegear/) — arrows, bolts, sling stones, and other ammunition.
- [Miscellaneous Gear](/sohl/miscgear/) — tools, rations, light sources, climbing equipment, and the rest of the adventuring miscellany.
- [Containers](/sohl/containergear/) — packs, pouches, bags, and waterskins.
- [Concoctions](/sohl/concoctiongear/) — potions, poisons, alchemical preparations, and ritual reagents.
