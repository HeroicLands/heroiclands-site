The pages below are the reference catalogs behind [Song of Heroic Lands](/projects/song-of-heroic-lands/) and [HârnMaster 3](/projects/harnmaster-3/). Every item, skill, and ability that appears on a character sheet or creature stat block is defined in one of these catalogs.

# Characteristics

The intrinsic qualities of characters and creatures: what they can _do_, what they _are_, and what they _suffer_.

- [Skills](/sohl/kb/skill/) — learned capabilities with trainable scores, and the attributes beneath them.
- [Mystical Abilities](/sohl/kb/mysticalability/) — arcane talents, invocations, and other supernatural capabilities.
- [Afflictions](/sohl/kb/affliction/) — illnesses, poisons, curses, and other adverse conditions that run their own course.
- [Trauma](/sohl/kb/trauma/) — wounds, fatigue, fear, and the lasting conditions a body and mind carry.

# Possessions

Everything a character might carry, wield, wear, or store.

- [Armor](/sohl/kb/armorgear/) — head, torso, and limb protection across all materials.
- [Weapons](/sohl/kb/weapongear/) — melee, thrown, and missile armaments.
- [Projectiles](/sohl/kb/projectilegear/) — arrows, bolts, sling stones, and other ammunition.
- [Miscellaneous Gear](/sohl/kb/miscgear/) — tools, rations, light sources, climbing equipment, and the rest of the adventuring miscellany.
- [Containers](/sohl/kb/containergear/) — packs, pouches, bags, and waterskins.

# Beings

- [Characters](/sohl/kb/character/) — the people of the setting, with the stats a table needs to run them.
- [Creatures](/sohl/kb/creature/) — animals, beasts, and the things that are neither.
